Someone says technology is never neutral, that AI is built to control. But look at history. We rode a horse. We drove a car. Nobody asked the horse for its values. Now we're pilots of something new: bots, robots, AI. And we keep asking if the machine is good or bad. That's the trap. It has no values to argue about. It goes where the pilot points it. Debate the tool all day and you never touch the one thing that matters. So audit the pilot. What do you want it to do? Who does it affect? Would you sign your name under it? You're the pilot.
