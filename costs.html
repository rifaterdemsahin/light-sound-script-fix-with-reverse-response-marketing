Free (runs locally)

  • Whisper (word timestamps)
  • ffmpeg (voice clean, mix, loudness)
  • Remotion (graphics, subtitles, render)
  • Thumbnail text and Skool end card

Where the money went

  • Animation is the biggest line: about 60% of spend. Two hero clips at $0.04 each.
  • Mistakes cost $0.06: two stretched landscape clips, one test image and one voiceover redone to add the community name.

How to spend less next time

  • Animate one hero shot, not two. Saves $0.04.
  • Test each new fal model on one image first. Check the output size (9:16) before running the second clip.
  • Lock the script, including the CTA, before generating the voice. Each redo costs about $0.014.
  • Generate one music bed and reuse it across Shorts. It costs about $0.012 and loops.
  • Make sound effects once and keep them in a shared folder. Two generations cost $0.02.
  • Images are already the cheapest part: flux/schnell at 4 steps is about $0.003 each.
Not counted: your time, Azure or other hosting, and any YouTube or Skool fees. The thumbnail background image is counted above.
